## Critical Findings and Corrections

- A14: removed the false implication that 50 km/h is generally permitted in a residential area; the scenario now states only that 50 km/h is permitted on the road concerned and still requires speed adaptation.
- A21: replaced blanket mandatory-stop wording with the exact duty to approach at moderate speed and give way to pedestrians on or about to enter the crossing, stopping when necessary.
- A25: removed the incorrect claim that cyclists waiting or preparing to cross have the same priority as pedestrians. The text now distinguishes users already on the crossing from users entering it.
- A31: removed the blanket claim that all temporary signs replace ordinary signs. The precedence rule is limited to orange longitudinal markings over white longitudinal markings at the same location; authorised-person instructions remain superior.
- A53: changed all content from a current-law presentation to a future-law presentation with the verified 1 June 2027 entry-into-force date.
- A3: removed the unsupported statement that coasting in neutral is itself prohibited; it is now presented only as unsafe driving advice.
- A47: removed absolute or penalty claims not supported by the reviewed provisions and clarified multi-track level-crossing conduct.
- A43: removed an unsupported "absolute priority" formulation and aligned the text with the direct prohibition on entering while a rail vehicle approaches.

## Major Findings and Corrections

- Aligned all 34 names with the official Dutch and French meanings and faithful English and Arabic translations.
- Corrected A14 to cover raised devices rather than only a speed bump.
- Removed A29 advice to leave the engine idling while livestock crosses; the governed action is to slow or stop when necessary, avoid startling the animals, and follow the handler's directions.
- Removed the unsupported A31 implication that the road remains open; the content now states only the verified works hazard and possible temporary layout.
- Replaced absolute reactions in A35 with controlled, situation-dependent driving guidance.
- Aligned A39 overtaking wording with the limited-visibility rule for a two-way road.
- Corrected A33 red and amber traffic-light explanations and the precedence of authorised persons.
- Corrected A41 crossing conditions and A45/A47 placement: at or in the immediate vicinity of the level crossing.
- Replaced the generic A49 "tramway crossing" label with the official scope: one or more tracks laid in the carriageway crossing the public road.
- Replaced unsupported penalty and licence-loss wording with the actual rule or safety action.
- Rewrote the DANGER lesson so it no longer claims every danger sign is triangular or every sign is placed a few metres before the hazard.

## Minor Findings and Corrections

- Standardised terminology and punctuation in all four languages.
- Removed repeated generic explanations and replaced them with sign-specific educational text.
- Improved hard-question scenarios while preserving importer choice-count rules.
